`flipper_decode_secplus1` implements Security+1.0 as a 40-bit binary frame
with a 4-bit popcount checksum. The real protocol uses 42 ternary symbols
(`BIT_0/1/2` = 3T/2T/1T low pulses) over two packets and has no checksum field.
The Flipper-ARF reference at `lib/subghz/protocols/secplus_v1.c` includes an
encoder and decoder. This implementation refuses a frame built to that
specification. The row above shows the raw result; the roughly 3% of signals
that pass are chance matches to the checksum gate, not successful decodes.

Fixing this requires rewriting the decoder for the ternary format and checking
it against a real capture. The generator cannot produce that validation data:
the protocol has no checksum to compute.

AUTO uses `flipper_decode()` and only the `auto_safe` decoders; this is the
default path. FRC uses `flipper_decode_ex()` after the protocol has been
selected. A force-only protocol's 0% AUTO score reflects the registry policy,
not a decoder failure; see the `auto_safe` field in `FLIPPER_DECODERS`.
