Security+1.0: flipper_decode_secplus1 implements a 40-bit binary model with a
4-bit popcount checksum. The real protocol is 42 TERNARY symbols (BIT_0/1/2 =
3T/2T/1T low pulses) over two packets, with no checksum field. Reference:
Flipper-ARF lib/subghz/protocols/secplus_v1.c (encoder and decoder). A frame
built to that spec is refused by this decoder. Its row above is the raw figure,
and the ~3% that pass are chance matches of the checksum gate, not decodes.
Fixing it means rewriting the decoder to the ternary spec, which needs a real
capture to validate against; the generator cannot produce one, because there
is no checksum to compute.

AUTO  = flipper_decode(), auto_safe decoders only (the default path).
FRC   = flipper_decode_ex() with the protocol forced (after selecting it).
A force-only protocol scoring 0% in AUTO is the registry policy, not a
decoder failure: see the auto_safe field in FLIPPER_DECODERS.
